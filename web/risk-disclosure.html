Read carefully. Trading financial markets — including crypto, forex, commodities, and indices — carries substantial risk. You may lose some or all of your capital.

1. Market Risk

Prices can move sharply and unexpectedly in response to news, economic events, or liquidity changes. Past performance does not guarantee future results. No system — including ROBOMLM — can predict markets with certainty.

2. Execution Risk

Orders may be delayed, partially filled, or rejected by exchanges. Slippage can occur. System outages, network delays, or broker failures may prevent you from entering or exiting positions.

3. Automation Risk

AUTOROBOMLM and Mission Mode place trades automatically based on configured rules. If configured incorrectly, or if market conditions change, the system may place trades you did not intend. Always monitor your account and use the Kill Switch when needed.

4. Data Accuracy

Market data comes from third-party sources (Bybit, Dhan, and others). While we aim for accuracy, ROBOMLM does not guarantee that prices, indicators, or signals are error-free.

5. No Financial Advice

ROBOMLM is a technical tool, not a financial advisor. Nothing displayed in this application constitutes investment advice. You are solely responsible for your trading decisions.

6. PAPER vs LIVE Mode

PAPER mode simulates trades without real money. LIVE mode places real orders with real capital. Always test your strategies in PAPER mode first.
